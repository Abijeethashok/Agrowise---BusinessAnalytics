    Macro-F1

    Log-loss (quality of the probability scores)

    Lower is better. Decision-tree probabilities are crude, which is why they score poorly here even when accuracy looks fine.

    Robustness to noisy inputs

    Gaussian noise scaled by each input's standard deviation was added to the test inputs and clipped to physically valid ranges. This is a simulation, not measured field error.

    Feature importance (selected model)

    Permutation importance on the test set: how much accuracy drops when one input is shuffled. Correlated inputs can share importance, and a low value describes this model and dataset, not agronomy.

    Confusion matrix (held-out test set)

    Rows are the true crop and columns the predicted crop. Green diagonal cells are correct predictions; red cells are errors.
