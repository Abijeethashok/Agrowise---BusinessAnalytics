The problem

Choosing a crop without considering nutrient levels, soil pH, temperature, humidity and rainfall can lead to poor decisions and inefficient use of resources. AgroWise turns an agricultural dataset into a clear, explainable comparison, so those conditions are checked before a decision is made.

What AgroWise does

Crop recommendation

A machine-learning model trained on the dataset ranks the three crops whose records best match your conditions.

Selected-crop check

See whether the evidence gives strong, moderate or low support for the crop you chose, and which better-matching alternatives exist.

Reference profiles

Each of your seven values is compared with the crop's typical range in the data, so you can see exactly where it differs.

Analytics dashboard

Statistical tests, correlations and model performance, all calculated from the real dataset and saved results.

Read the scores honestly

Scores describe how closely your conditions resemble crop records in the dataset. They are not the probability that a crop will grow successfully or give a particular yield.

The dataset contains no soil texture, growth stages, yields or fertilizer quantities, so AgroWise does not give those. It is a decision-support prototype, not agronomic advice.
